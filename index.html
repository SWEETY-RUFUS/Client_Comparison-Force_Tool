Built-in Force mapping

The output workbook includes a Force sheet for temporary client-by-client mapping. No separate mapping upload is needed.

  1. Download the comparison result and open it in Excel.
  2. In the Force sheet, paste Client_Unique_ID and PTY_LOCAL_CODE into columns A and B.
  3. Type the exact client worksheet name in cell D2, for example 286859_Pernod.
  4. Excel will calculate PTY_LOCAL_CODE only for that selected client sheet, using its Matched_CL_ID.
  5. To map another client, clear columns A:B, change D2 to the next sheet name, and paste the next mapping.
🔒 Your file stays in your browser. The workbook is processed locally and is not uploaded to this website by the comparison tool.
